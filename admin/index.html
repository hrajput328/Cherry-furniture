💾 How to Update Your Website

This admin panel saves all your changes locally. To apply them to your live website, follow these 3 steps.

Step 1 — Export Data

Click the button below to download your cherry-data.json file. This file contains all your products, business info, orders, and settings.

Step 2 — Place the File

Put the downloaded cherry-data.json file in the same folder as your cherry-furniture-website.html file.

📁 Your folder:
cherry-furniture-website.html
cherry-data.json ← new file here

Step 3 — Upload to Netlify

Drag your entire folder (HTML + JSON) to netlify.com again. Your website will update in 30 seconds.
